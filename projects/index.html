Real jobs from our crews in Hampton Roads — from tear-out and drying to the finished floor.

Kitchen, Hallway & Entry Water Damage Restoration

Water spread under the flooring through the kitchen, hallway, and front entry. We removed the wet flooring and carpet down to the slab, set up containment, dried everything with commercial equipment, treated the exposed slab, and installed new luxury vinyl plank flooring.

1Tear-outWet flooring, carpet & baseboard removed
2ContainmentPlastic barriers + HEPA air scrubber
3DryingDehumidifier & air movers, checked daily
4TreatmentExposed slab treated before rebuild
5RebuildNew LVP flooring & trim installed

Kitchen

The flooring was removed to the concrete slab so it could dry properly. A commercial dehumidifier and air mover ran until the area was dry.

Hallway

Plastic containment kept dust and moisture out of the rest of the home while air movers and a HEPA air scrubber ran.

Front Entry

The entry was taken down to the slab, cleaned, dried, and treated before the new floor went in.
