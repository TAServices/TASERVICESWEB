Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency water damage service in Virginia Beach?

Yes. Call (757) 567-8687 any time, day or night, for burst pipes, flooding, appliance leaks, or storm water. We serve Virginia Beach and the surrounding Hampton Roads cities.

Do you work with my homeowner's insurance?

Yes. We document the damage with photos and moisture readings and help you through the claim process so it moves faster.

What should I do right after water damage?

If it is safe, shut off the water source and electricity to affected areas, move valuables off wet floors, and call us. The sooner drying starts, the less chance of mold and structural damage.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free and there is no obligation.
