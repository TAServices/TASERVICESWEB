Professional Mold Removal for Hampton Roads Homes

Coastal Virginia’s humidity, frequent storms, and older crawl-space homes make mold a common problem in Virginia Beach, Norfolk, and Chesapeake. Mold usually means there’s a moisture problem — and it gets worse until both the mold and the moisture are dealt with.

Signs you may need mold remediation

  • A musty or earthy smell that won’t go away
  • Spots or discoloration on drywall, ceilings, baseboards, or in closets
  • Recent water damage, leaks, or a damp crawl space
  • Condensation on windows or high indoor humidity

Our Mold Remediation Process

1. Inspection & moisture source

We inspect affected areas, use moisture meters to find where water is coming from, and determine how far growth has spread.

2. Containment

Work areas are sealed off with plastic barriers and negative air pressure so spores don’t spread to the rest of your home.

3. HEPA air filtration

HEPA air scrubbers capture airborne spores during and after the work.

4. Removal & cleaning

Contaminated porous materials like drywall and insulation are removed and bagged; framing and other surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial.

5. Drying & prevention

We dry the area, address the moisture cause, and recommend steps to keep mold from coming back.

Mold After Water Damage

Mold can begin growing within 24–48 hours after a leak or flood. If you’ve had recent water damage, fast professional drying is the best way to prevent a mold problem in the first place.

Mold Remediation FAQs

How do I know if I have mold?

Common signs are a musty smell, visible spots or discoloration on walls, ceilings, or baseboards, peeling paint, and recent leaks or high humidity. If you smell it but can’t see it, it may be hidden inside walls or under floors.

Can I just clean mold with bleach?

Bleach can lighten surface staining, but it doesn’t fix porous materials like drywall or wood, and it doesn’t solve the moisture problem. Disturbing larger areas of mold without containment can also spread spores through the house.

Why does mold keep coming back?

Mold returns when the moisture source isn’t fixed. We find and address the leak, humidity, or water damage behind it so the problem stays gone.

Is mold remediation covered by insurance?

It depends on your policy and the cause. Mold resulting from a sudden covered water loss is often covered; mold from long-term leaks or humidity often isn’t. We document everything to support your claim.
