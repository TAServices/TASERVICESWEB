Emergency Water Removal & Drying

Water damage gets worse by the hour. Drywall wicks water upward, wood floors cup, and mold can start growing in 24–48 hours. Our crews respond 24/7 across Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads to stop the damage early and dry your home the right way — not just until it looks dry.

Common causes we handle

  • Burst or leaking pipes and supply lines
  • Water heater, washing machine, dishwasher, and refrigerator leaks
  • Toilet and sink overflows
  • Roof leaks and storm or hurricane water intrusion
  • Flooded crawl spaces and slab leaks

Our Water Damage Restoration Process

1. Assessment & moisture mapping

We find the source, use moisture meters to map exactly how far the water spread (often well beyond what you can see), and set drying goals for your home.

2. Water extraction & tear-out

We remove standing water and, with your approval, remove materials that can’t be saved — such as wet insulation, saturated drywall (flood cuts), or damaged flooring.

3. Structural drying

Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers dry the structure itself — framing, subfloor, and walls. We take readings every day so drying is verified, not guessed.

4. Cleaning, antimicrobial & odor control

Affected areas are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial as needed to prevent mold growth and odors.

5. Documentation & restoration

You get photos and moisture logs for your insurance claim, and we help put your home back to pre-loss condition.

Help With Your Insurance Claim

Most sudden and accidental water damage is covered by homeowner’s insurance. We document everything adjusters need — photos, moisture readings, and a clear scope of work — to help your claim move faster.

What To Do Right Now

  • Shut off the main water valve if you can do it safely.
  • Turn off power to wet areas — never walk through standing water near outlets.
  • Move furniture, rugs, and valuables off wet floors.
  • Take photos for insurance, then call us at (757) 567-8687.

Water Damage Restoration FAQs

Do you offer 24/7 emergency water damage service?

Yes — we respond day or night for burst pipes, flooding, appliance leaks, or storm-related water intrusion across Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Suffolk, and the rest of Hampton Roads.

How fast can you be on site?

Response times vary by location and call volume, but we aim to arrive as quickly as possible anywhere in the region. Call us and we’ll give you a realistic arrival time.

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. We provide detailed documentation, photos, and moisture readings to support your claim, and we work with many carriers.

How long does it take to dry a house after water damage?

Most homes take about 3 to 5 days of professional drying, depending on how much water there was, what materials got wet, and how quickly drying started. We take daily moisture readings and only pull equipment when materials are verified dry.

What is your water damage process?

Assessment and moisture mapping, water extraction, removal of non-salvageable materials (as authorized), structural drying with professional dehumidifiers and air movers, antimicrobial application as needed, and final verification.

Yes — contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate.
