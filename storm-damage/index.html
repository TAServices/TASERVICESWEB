Storm, Wind & Flood Damage Cleanup

Hampton Roads sees hurricanes, nor’easters, tropical storms, and flash flooding. When a storm damages your home, the first priority is stopping more water from getting in — then drying and restoring what’s already wet before mold sets in.

Storm damage services

  • Emergency roof tarping and board-up
  • Water extraction after roof leaks, wind-driven rain, or flooding
  • Structural drying with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers
  • Removal of wet drywall, insulation, and flooring
  • Cleanup, antimicrobial treatment, and odor control
  • Photo and moisture documentation for insurance

After the Storm: What To Do

  • Stay safe — avoid downed power lines and standing water near electricity.
  • Photograph all damage before anything is moved or thrown out.
  • Prevent further damage if you can do so safely (buckets, tarps, moving valuables).
  • Call us at (757) 567-8687 for emergency tarping, water removal, and drying.

Why Speed Matters

Wet building materials can start growing mold within 24–48 hours. Our water damage restoration process gets drying started fast, and if mold has already appeared we handle mold remediation too.

Storm Damage Cleanup FAQs

Do you provide emergency board-up and tarping?

Yes. After storms, wind, or fallen trees we can tarp roofs and board up openings to keep more water out and secure your home.

Can you help after a hurricane or tropical storm?

Yes. We handle water removal, drying, tear-out, and cleanup after hurricanes, nor’easters, and tropical storms across Hampton Roads. Call early — demand spikes after major storms.

Does homeowner’s insurance cover storm damage?

Wind and rain damage is often covered; rising flood water usually requires separate flood insurance. We document the damage thoroughly to support whichever claim applies.

Are estimates free?

Yes — contact us for a free, no-obligation estimate.
